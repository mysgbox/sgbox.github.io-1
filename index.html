SGbox – Smart Grid Box

From a connected home to an intelligent neighborhood — with SGbox, energy becomes collective.

See the Prototype

Problem & Solution

The Current Problem

In Morocco, surplus solar energy is often wasted. Storage is expensive, and there is no energy coordination between neighbors, which hinders autonomy and the valuation of local renewable resources.

Our Solution: SGbox

SGbox is an open-source smart box that transforms each house into an active node of a local mini-grid. It optimizes production, consumption, storage, and enables electricity sharing between neighbors for collective and sustainable energy.

How It Works

1. Data Collection

Measures electrical and environmental parameters and integrates local weather forecasts.

2. Smart Analysis

A local LLM (Ollama) and EMHASS enable contextual decision-making and predict production/consumption.

3. Optimization & Control

Node-RED orchestrates real-time load scheduling and energy sharing between homes.

4. User Interface

A Home Assistant dashboard provides a complete overview and secure control, even remotely.

Prototype & Validation

The current prototype simulates a network of two interconnected houses.

  • Supervision: Raspberry Pi and ESP32 for IoT communication.
  • Measurements: Current/voltage sensors, PV panels, and Li-Ion batteries.
  • Alternative Storage: Innovative thermal reservoir using fine sand (proof of concept).
  • Access: Secure remote connection for real-time monitoring and control.

Impact, Innovation & Vision

Ecological Impact

Reduces energy waste and enhances solar production with open-source, repairable hardware.

Technological Innovation

A novel fusion of IoT, local AI (cloud-free), and predictive optimization in a distributed, collaborative architecture.

Social Impact

Empowers neighborhood energy autonomy and democratizes technology through an open-source, participatory approach.

Our Long-Term Vision

To establish SGbox as a Moroccan open-source standard for smart microgrids, contributing to a local, sustainable, and citizen-centric energy transition.
